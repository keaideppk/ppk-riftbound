# Riftbound · 界隙行者

[简体中文](README.zh-CN.md) · [Play in your browser](https://keaideppk.github.io/riftbound/)

Riftbound is a side-scrolling 2D Metroidvania you can play in your browser. On the seventh night after the gates went silent, a distress signal arrives in your own voice. Set out from Riftgate Station and recover three seals from the Iron Mines, Thorn Castle, and Mycelial Ruins to trace the last signal to its source.

## Play

- **Online.** Open the [GitHub Pages build](https://keaideppk.github.io/riftbound/) and select **踏入界隙** on the title screen. The in-game interface is in Simplified Chinese.
- **Offline.** Open the [index.html file page](index.html), select **Download raw file**, and open the downloaded file in your browser. The standalone HTML includes the game's procedurally drawn scenery, characters, effects, and synthesized audio.

## Exploration and Combat

- **18 connected rooms and four boss fights.** Follow the main route or climb to upper platforms for supplies, logs, and health shards.
- **Three movement upgrades.** Dash across broken bridges, grapple to anchor rings, and double-jump to distant ledges. Chain two grapples to reach the Thorn Warden's door above Crown Stairway. You can also grapple toward regular enemies to close the gap for a melee attack.
- **Walk-through passages.** Walk into a passage marked `↔` to enter the next room. Press `E` at gates between worlds. Room arrivals and beacons have safe areas.
- **Beacons and saves.** Press `E` at a beacon to restore health and set your respawn point. Open the map while near a beacon to fast-travel to one you have activated. Progress is stored in your browser. To resume an online run, use the same browser and site.

## Controls

| Action | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Move | `A` / `D` or `←` / `→` |
| Jump | `Space`, `Z`, or `W` |
| Attack | `J` or `X`. Press again during a Walker's Blade swing to follow up. |
| Switch weapons | `1`–`4` to select, `F` to cycle, or click the weapon bar |
| Dash | `Shift` or `K` once unlocked |
| Grapple | `V` once unlocked. The touch button is `↗`. |
| Interact | `E` or `↑` |
| Heal | `Q` or `C`, costs 3 energy |
| Map | `M` or `Tab` |
| Pause | `Esc` |

Phones and tablets have on-screen controls. You can also enable them under **操作与设置** (Controls and Settings). For offline saves, keep using the same file location and a browser that supports local storage for files.

## Weapons

| Weapon | Where to find it | How it plays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Walker's Blade (行者刃) | Starting weapon | Two-hit combo that restores energy on hit |
| Coil Crossbow (线圈弩) | Pressure Pump Room (泄压泵房) | Assisted aim, one energy per shot. Bolts stop at terrain. |
| Thorn Spear (荆棘枪) | Rootbound Chapel (覆根礼拜堂) | Long thrust that pushes back regular enemies and restores energy on hit |
| Echo Hammer (回声锤) | Echo Nursery (回声培养巢) | Slow sweep that hits on both sides and clears nearby enemy projectiles |

Collected weapons and the equipped weapon are saved. Older saves convert the wall jump into the Thorn Grapple and keep the room's weapon unlocks.
